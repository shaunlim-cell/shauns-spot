FLOWS TO TRY

  1. CRM icon → Leaderboard (lands on Daily · Last period · Winnings)
  2. Switch Daily/Weekly, use the ‹ › period arrows, and switch Winnings/Multiplier (bottom toggle in portrait, left rail in landscape). The other selections stay, and the list jumps to the top.
  3. Landscape: only the rows column on the right scrolls; the rule line and top 3 stay put.
  4. Scroll down and tap a replay. It opens full screen with no header; the ✕ at the top-right closes it at any time and returns you to the same scroll position.
  5. Press SPIN, watch it through to the result, then Watch again. Buy Bonus, bet ± and auto-spin are greyed out.
  6. Tap a top-3 image or a row's game name to get the switch-game confirmation. Delisted games (grey ↗) can't be tapped; a grey ▶ means no replay. Tick “Top-3 edge cases” to see both on the cards.
  7. Tap the game you're already playing (Golden Wish at the start) to see the “already in this game” popup; CONFIRM just closes it.
  8. Turn on the failure options and VND/Thai, and switch to landscape or a short screen.
