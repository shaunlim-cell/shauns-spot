1 · Which side of the line are you on?

Usable width ÷ height, as a single number. Left of 1.125 → Narrow, right → Wide.

2 · Why exactly 1.125?

Both layouts are tested at the current size. Whichever ends up bigger on screen wins. 1.125 (= 1830 ÷ 1626) is where they tie.

What this means for wireframes

Narrow canvas ≈ phone × 2.6–3On an iPhone SE the narrow canvas shrinks to ~0.32×. Draw body text at ~40–44px and tap targets at ~135px on the 990 canvas.
Leftover space needs a specProportions are locked, so mismatched screens get empty bands (hatched). Define a background for them.
Height counts, not just widthSame width, different height can flip the layout. Try the "SE + keyboard" preset to see a phone jump to Wide.
